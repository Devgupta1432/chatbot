Integration

Add to any website
in 3 steps

No developer needed. Works on WordPress, Wix, Shopify, custom HTML — anything that lets you add a script tag.

1

Open your Pixora Dashboard

Log in and fill in your business details — name, logo, brand colour, services, FAQs, working hours.

Open Dashboard →
2

Click Save — copy your script

After saving, your unique script tag is generated automatically. One click to copy it.

3

Paste before </body> on your site

That's it. The chatbot appears instantly — branded with your colours, your logo, your info.

Works on every platform:
WordPress Shopify Wix Webflow HTML
index.html
<!-- Your website HTML -->
<html>
  <head>…</head>
  <body>
    <!-- your content -->

    <!-- Pixora chatbot ↓ -->
    <script src="https://pixora-chatbot.netlify.app/widget-v1.js?id=your-widget-id"></script>

  </body>
</html>
YOUR SCRIPT TAG
Replace YOUR_ID with your client ID from the dashboard.
